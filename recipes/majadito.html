Majadito

Description

Majadito is a traditional dish from Santa Cruz. The original version does not include beef. (Wikipedia)

This dish is a family favorite because it's hearty and filling. Majadito combines rice, beef jerky (cheque), and a mix of vegetables to create a tasty and nutritious meal. The special way of cooking makes each ingredient taste amazing. (easybreezybites.com)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • 1/2 pound beef jerky (cheque)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 ripe plantains
  • Optional: peas carrots, or other vegetables

Steps

  1. Soak the beef jerky in hot water for about 30 minutes to soften it. Drain and shred the beef into small pieces.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion, garlic, tomatoes, and bell peppers. Cook until the vegetables are soft and fragrant.
  3. Stir in the shredded beef jerky and rice. Cook for a few minutes, allowing the rice to absorb the flavors from the vegetables and beef.
  4. Pour in the beef broth, add salt and pepper to taste, and bring it to a boil. Reduce the heat to low, cover the pan, and let it simmer until the rice is fully cooked and has absorbed most of the liquid, about 20 minutes.
  5. In a separate pan, fry the eggs sunny-side up. Peel and slice the plantains, then fry them until they are golden brown and crispy.
  6. Serve the Majadito hot, topped with a fried egg and accompanied by fried plantains.
